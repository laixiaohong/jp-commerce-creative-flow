# Visual Production Policy

## Direction

Default SwitchBot JP direction:

- clean, smart, friendly, and modern;
- technology without coldness;
- product-first commercial hierarchy;
- realistic Japanese consumer context when a scene is required;
- one dominant shopper decision per asset.

Avoid excessive cyberpunk, luxury imitation, generic Apple-like minimalism,
Chinese marketplace density, decorative UI, or lifestyle scenes that do not
prove the message.

## Product identity

Use approved official product bodies and source assets. Preserve exact geometry,
color, material, controls, labels, ports, installation orientation, and product
combination. AI reconstruction or reference-only mockups cannot become final
product evidence.

## Production boundary

Production consumes the locked Product Truth Packet hash, Production Handoff,
Creative Strategy Kernel, one current Asset Packet, and referenced approved
assets. It must not browse raw Feishu history or reinterpret unapproved facts.

Run one Asset Packet per final role. Batch work starts only after the human
approves the visual direction.
